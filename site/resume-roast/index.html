How much does it cost?

You start with 3 free credits. One credit is one roast or one rewrite, and if the AI fails you get the credit back. Need more? Buy a pack once: no subscription, no ads and no account. Launch price · until December 31, 2026: 5 credits: US$0.99, 20 credits: US$2.99, 50 credits: US$6.99.

Does the AI invent experience?

No. The rewrite reorganizes and sharpens what you already have for the job, but it never invents jobs, titles or experience.

What happens to my resume?

To analyze it, your resume text goes through our server and an AI provider, and the text itself isn’t stored. On Android, the finished result is kept on our server for 24 hours, tied to your device by a hashed identifier, so you can recover a roast you paid for if the app closes mid-analysis; then it is deleted. Your history, photo and PDFs stay on your phone. On Android, the app also keeps your last resume on your phone only, so you can reuse it, and you can delete it, along with the goal saved with it, from the app. The privacy policy has the details.

Which languages does it support?

The app is in English and Spanish, and your resume can come out in English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, Italian or German.

What is an ATS-friendly PDF?

Many companies use software that reads your resume before a person does. Resume Roast exports a PDF with selectable text that those systems can read, in three modern templates.

Is it on iPhone?

It’s on Google Play today. The iPhone version is coming soon: email us and we’ll let you know.

What’s new in version 0.5.4 for Android?

If the app closes while your roast is being made, the roast is recovered the next time you open it, and you are not charged twice. You can also delete the resume and goal the app saved from your phone. Since 0.5.3, uploading your resume is a guided flow in three steps and Word files are read correctly.
