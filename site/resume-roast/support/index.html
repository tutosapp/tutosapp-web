Resume Roast: support

Last updated: November 16, 2026

On this page

  1. Contact
  2. Frequently asked questions

Resume Roast is the AI that tells you what recruiters only think. Paste your CV, or upload a PDF, Word file or photo, and get an honest score out of 100, the red flags, concrete fixes and, if you add a job posting, how well you fit it. Then get your CV rewritten for that job and export it as a PDF.

Available for Android on Google Play and coming soon for iPhone on the App Store.

Contact

Write to hola@tutosapp.co. We usually answer within two business days, in English or Spanish.

It helps to include your app version: in the app, open About and tap the version line to copy it.

Frequently asked questions

How do credits work?

Your first 3 credits are free. One credit is one roast or one rewrite. If the AI fails, the credit comes back. Need more? Buy a pack once (5, 20 or 50 credits): no subscription, no ads and no account. Credits don't expire.

I bought credits but my balance didn't change.

Keep the app open with an internet connection for a few seconds, or close it and open it again: a purchase that was paid but not yet added is added on the next launch. If it still doesn't show, write to us with the date of the purchase and your app version.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases on iPhone are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, see Google Play's refund options.

Do I lose my credits if I delete the app?

On iPhone, credits belong to the device, not to an account. They stay if you delete and reinstall the app on the same iPhone. They don't move to a new phone or between iPhone and Android.

How do I upload my CV?

On Android (version 0.5.3 or later), the app guides you in three steps. First, choose how to give us your CV: upload a file, paste the text, or use the last one you loaded, which is saved only on your phone. Then, if you want, add the role you are going for or a job posting. Last, review everything and the credit cost before you start.

The app closed while my roast was being made.

On Android (version 0.5.4 or later), open the app again: the roast is recovered and you are not charged twice.

What happens to my CV?

There's no sign-up. Your CV text is sent over an encrypted connection to our AI provider, analyzed, and the text itself is not saved on our servers. On Android, the finished result is kept on our server for 24 hours, tied to your device by a hashed identifier, so you can recover a review you paid for if the app closes mid-analysis; it is then deleted, and you can ask us to delete it sooner. Your history, your photo and your PDFs stay on your device. On Android, the last CV you loaded also stays on your phone only, so you can use it again. Details are in the privacy policy.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, open History and clear it; deleting the app removes everything stored on the phone. On Android, to delete the last CV the app keeps for you, tap Delete this resume from my phone in the first step of uploading your CV. From version 0.5.4, this also deletes the role, job posting and language saved with it, and the app asks you to confirm first if a roast you already paid for is still pending. To delete the credit balance kept for your device on our servers, write to us.

Which file types can I upload?

PDF, Word (.docx), and photos or scans of your CV (up to 6 files at once). You can also paste the text. If a Word file could not be read on Android, update the app: version 0.5.3 fixed it.

In which languages does it work?

The app is in English and Spanish. Your roast and your rewritten CV can be in English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, Italian or German.

The result looks wrong or offensive.

The feedback is written by AI and can make mistakes. Tell us at hola@tutosapp.co with what you saw, and we'll look into it.
