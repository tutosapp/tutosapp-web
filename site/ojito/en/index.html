What it does for you

  • An answer for everyone

    What works for you may not work for your baby. Ojito gives you your result and, below it, one for each person in your home by age.

  • Allergies and conditions

    Flag peanuts, dairy, gluten, diabetes or high blood pressure, among others, and Ojito warns you when it sees them on the label.

  • Built for Colombia

    The score looks at Colombian warning labels, additives and how processed the product is. For personal care it checks ingredients against Andean Community and European Union lists.

  • No account, no paid placement

    No sign-up, and no names or photos of your children. No brand pays to appear or to improve its score.

Which products does it work with?

Packaged food and personal-care products with a barcode, including those with no nutrition table. If a product is missing, take a photo of the label: the ingredients and, if it has one, the nutrition table. Ojito’s AI reads them.

What about products with no barcode?

You can rate those too. Take photos of the product and Ojito shows you what it thinks it is; you confirm with "Is this your product?" and get your result, just like any other. If a photo does not work, the app tells you what to do and you can try again.

Does it rate salt, oils, protein or powders?

Yes. Salt, oils and other basics are rated without asking for more photos. Protein and collagen are too, with a notice that they are supplements. Powders are rated as you prepare them, and the result tells you how.

What if the app is closed while I send photos?

Photos left pending are not lost if the system closes the app: when you open it again, you can carry on.

Who reviews a product I send in?

Ojito’s AI reviews the photos on its own. A person on the team only sees them when the AI can’t decide or the case is sensitive, such as allergens or baby food. The front photo published as the product’s image may be reviewed by the team afterwards, and it removes the ones that should not be there.

How does it know what’s good for my baby?

It only asks for their age, never a name or photos. Below your result you then see a line for your baby, for pregnancy or breastfeeding, or for another adult at home, with the reason in a few words.

Does Ojito replace a pediatrician or doctor?

No. Ojito is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease. It helps you read the label. No alert doesn’t mean the product is free of that allergen: always check the package.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and scan. Your family’s details stay on your phone.

How much does it cost?

Scanning, the result, the reasons, your family’s alerts and other options cost nothing. An optional subscription, Ojito Familia, will later add full history and a shopping list.

Where can I get it?

Ojito is on the App Store for iPhone. On Android it’s in testing and coming soon to Google Play: to try it early, email us and we’ll add you to the testing group.

What’s new in version 0.1.20?
