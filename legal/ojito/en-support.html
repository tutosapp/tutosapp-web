Ojito support

Something not working, a product looks wrong, or a question about the app? Write to us at hola@tutosapp.co. We answer in Spanish and English.

If a product looks wrong

On the product result, tap Something wrong? Let us know, choose what is wrong and, if you want, add a note and photos of the package. It goes to the Ojito team, who review it. You can also email us with the product barcode.

If the product has no barcode

Take photos of the product and Ojito shows you what it thinks it is. If it is yours, confirm with Is this your product? and you get your result. If it does not recognize it or gets it wrong, email us and we will look into it.

If a photo does not work, the app tells you what to do and you can try again. Pending photos are not lost if the system closes the app.

Salt, oils, protein and powders

Salt, oils and other basics are rated without asking for more photos. Protein and collagen are too, with a notice that they are supplements. Powders are rated as you prepare them, and the result tells you how.

Write to us from the app

In Settings, tap the option to send a report or suggestion. Your email app opens with a message to us that already includes the app's name, the app version (including build number), your phone model, the system version and the language, so that we can help you. You see it before sending and can delete it. The details are in the privacy policy. Settings also shows the version of Ojito you have installed.

Your data

To delete your installation identifier, go to Settings and tap Delete my data from the server. To ask us to delete your photos or notes, or for any question or complaint about your data, write to the same address. Who is responsible for your data and how we handle it is in the privacy policy.
